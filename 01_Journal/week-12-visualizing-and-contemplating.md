# Week 12 Journal: <Visualizing and Contemplating>

**Reading:** <Chapter 12: "Visualizing the Narrative">
**Date:** <2026-11-13>

While beginning chapter 12, "visualizing the narrative," It has become apparent to me that
visualizing the results of my data and what they mean are things I have yet to really consider.
I've sat with the data, compiled it, spent far too many hours simply looking at it, and although
I have begun to draw conclusions, the final picture is something I have rarely thought of. 
While reading this chapter, I begin to consider what kind of chart will best suit the data I 
have. Would I create a line chart to document how streams fluctuate for top streaming songs 
within the 4 month scope? If that was the only data I had, perhaps that would work. But with 
the TikTok video aspect of my data, there needs to bea chart that could account for that data 
as well as the Spotify data, right? Or can there be a chart for each, which would likely make 
it easier to conceptualize both as separate ideas, but might distract from the relationship
between the data and what connects them. The histogram seems like it might be the best option
possible for both, although multiple charts might best represent every aspect. Looking at these
different charts isn't necessarily unfamiliar, but the idea of doing it with my data and 
creating my own is definitely the foreign aspect. Especially when it comes to the coding of it
all, as the example coding lines shown in the chapter are... long and intimidating to say the 
least.
