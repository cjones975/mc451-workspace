# Week 02 Journal: Confusion Amongst the Numbers
**Reading:** Chapter 2: The Open Workspace
**Date:** <2026-09-04>

## Reflection
  I have never been a numbers person. Words have always been the most accessible form of information to me, 
but when that is mixed with numbers and data, I find myself getting confused and becoming less receptive
to the information I am given. Yet, despite my little amount of knowledge about the process of studies
and analytical research, I find it quite surprising how common it seems for published methods to not 
contain enough detail for an analysis to be re-run. It is a confusing concept to me, as one would assume
those doing research would take every preventatie measure to ensure the utmost level of detail is captured
so that their findings might come out as complete and accurate as possible. But that only proves the point
stated in the text: "the original research had never been built to be retraceable." Access to the
applications and systems out can help log and track every step made by a researcher, it makes me wonder
how data was lost if there are so many systems at the fingertips of those who need it. How was the data
being tracked and stored to be so easily lost or too vague to recover? 
  I have to say, I really am a bit nervous to get into all of this. Looking at all of the coding, systems, 
and software required just to get started is a bit overwhelming for someone like me who is all too 
unfamiliar with all of this! It’s like learning an entirely new language. I recognize my learning curve and 
I know I will have to really take my time when using these systems and I will likely have lots of questions 
fairly regularly (sorry in advance). Thank you for making the instructions so detailed and accessible though! 
Like I said, the words make it far easier to understand.
