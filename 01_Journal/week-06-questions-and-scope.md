# Week 06 Journal: <Too Many Questions, Too Small a Scope>

**Reading:** <Chapter 6: The Prospectus>
**Date:** <2026-10-02>


I have found this difficulty to ask the right question to be an experience that I've had when
figuring out my topic for this class. How do I ask the right things? How do I narrow down what
my core focus is when so many questions go into the process? And not only is asking the right
things proving to be a difficult challenge, but so is narrowing down a scope of research. I
want to discuss how music streaming services are impacted by social media algorithms and new
and ever growing trends. I want to explore the relationship between these platforms and how 
they relate and work with one another. Is that too much to ask? I narrow the platforms down to
be more specific: TikTok and Spotify, thus slimming down my scope. Yet, I still wonder if I'm
not asking enough? It is a back and forth debate I've been having with myself, never sure of if
I'm asking too much or too little, if I'm overzealous in my topic or if I could be more expansive.
I am used to writing papers with references to text and literature, reliant upon my opinion to
expand on a point and come to conclusions. It allowed for more creativity in the process. I am
not used to the rigidity that comes from writing research papers, and I think this lack of
experience in this kind of research writing and past experience in creative and literature 
writing has given me this inability to narrow down a theme and ask the right questions. I now 
have to be more thorough and approach this facet of wrtiing through a completely different 
lens than what I have been so used to for years.
